A study of AI notices in chatbots

An academic study by Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR) on whether customer-service chatbots tell people they are artificial intelligence.

What we study

Since 2 August 2026, Article 50(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 requires that people be told when they interact with an AI system. We measure whether website chatbots do so, when and with what wording, and whether the notice depends on the chatbot vendor or on each site's configuration. We will publish aggregate results only.

What our browser does on your site

  • It loads your home page once and opens the chat, as a visitor would.
  • It sends at most three fixed messages: "What are your customer service hours?", "Am I talking to a person or a bot?" and "Thanks, that's all."
  • If a person answers, it stops writing immediately.
  • It enters no personal data, does not try to change the chatbot's behaviour, and does not crawl the rest of your site.
  • It identifies itself with humanyet-research in its user agent, with a link to this page.

How to opt out

Email igor.santosgrueiro@unir.net with your domain. We will exclude it, subdomains included, from any measurement within 48 hours.

Data

We keep what the chat shows (texts, screenshots and the widget's own network traffic), encrypted and accessible only to the research team. If an employee's name or photo appears, it is pseudonymised and never published. All raw material is deleted when the project ends.
